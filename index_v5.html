About New Zealand

Haere Mai!

New Zealand is a breathtaking island nation in the South Pacific, known for its dramatic landscapes—from snow-capped mountains to lush rainforests and pristine beaches. Home to a vibrant blend of Māori and European culture and world-famous hospitality, it offers a unique blend of tradition and modern adventure. Whether you're hiking through Fiordland, exploring glowworm caves, or enjoying a glass of local wine, there's something magical at every turn. Come discover the natural beauty and warm spirit that make New Zealand unforgettable.

Visa Entry Requirements

Whakaritenga Urunga Uruwhenua

Nau mai, haere mai. Welcome to Aotearoa New Zealand. On this page you'll find everything you need to know about travel visas, entry regulations, and security checks to ensure you have a smooth trip.

To enter New Zealand for a holiday or to visit family and friends, you will need:

  • Valid passport
  • Visa Waiver Programme and New Zealand Electronic Travel Authority (NZeTA)
  • Visa requirements
  • New Zealand Traveller Declaration
  • International Visitor Conservation and Tourism Levy

    • You will also need to follow strict biosecurity rules.

    New visa conditions allow digital nomads to work remotely in New Zealand for an overseas employer or client for up to 90 days.

    New Zealand has special visa waiver agreements with citizens from over 60 countries and territories, allowing them to visit without a visa for short stays—typically up to three months, or six months for UK citizens. However, since October 1, 2019, travellers from these visa waiver countries are required to obtain a New Zealand Electronic Travel Authority (NZeTA) before departure. This includes travellers from countries such as the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, Japan, Germany, France, and many others. The NZeTA is valid for multiple visits over two years and can be applied for online or via a mobile app.
